How does booking work?

Pick a package, fill in the short form and send it via WhatsApp. Our team confirms availability and the final price, then guides you through payment.

Can I customise a package?

Absolutely. Add your wishes in the notes field and we will adjust hotels, activities and duration.

What is your cancellation policy?

Free cancellation up to 30 days before departure. Closer dates depend on supplier rules, which we explain before you pay.

Do you help with visas and insurance?

Yes. We provide document checklists, visa guidance and optional travel insurance.
